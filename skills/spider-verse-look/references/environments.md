# Environments: palettes and rules per location

Every set is a character statement ("This set is the first glimpse we get of Miles' soul", Demers, PDF 081) and a reduction exercise ("how far we could reduce the world around us", O'Keefe, PDF 118). Hex values are sampled from the scans (±10%). Full notes are in `book/`.

## New York City in general

- **"Our Manhattan is a caricature of the real one. We wanted to make sure that the sense of wonder you get when you stare up from the bottom of a skyscraper carries through"** (Yuhki Demers, PDF 018). Exaggerate the height; use extreme up and down cameras. **Manhattan lives on the cool side of the wheel ("cold and uninviting"); Brooklyn and home are warm** (PDF 018).
- **Night city (PDF 014, 018, 019):** building bodies near-black navy/violet (#151638 → #2c1738) with grids of warm lit windows (#f0d890, about half lit); the avenue fades to a pale violet-white glow at the vanishing point, so the distance is brighter than the foreground; 1–3 neon accents (hot magenta #ff40c0, lime #9ad020); vertical streak reflections on wet roads; a tiny Spider-Man placed in the brightest gap.
- **Pink dusk skyline from Brooklyn (Zac Retz, PDF 022–023):** flat pink sky #cc75a0 with floating patches of halftone dots; slabs stepping #dda2c0 → #c085a9 → #85628f toward the viewer; windows as tiny dot matrices; cool blue-grey rooftops #2a3246 in front with deep violet-black shadows; lime accents; floating ink ticks off building edges.
- **Daylight (Ross, Mullins, Chan; PDF 020):** facades abstracted into a patchwork of rectangles in cream #ece4cc, taupe #a89478, rose #a07a70, olive #8a8660; long hard flat shadows #2a2420; the distance washed to warm white. Irregular windows, not perfect grids.
- **Billboards as printed panels:** giant AM halftone portraits (blue-violet dots on white), ring-shaped CMY dots, diamond LED patterns, fictional ads ("THE DANGEROUS BLUE WOMAN", "TITANO VS POE LANDROVER") (PDF 021, 025–027).
- **Brooklyn blocks (Retz and Dalit elevation strips, PDF 010–011):** rows of 5–6-storey boxes in ochre, red or mustard brick with zig-zag fire escapes, cornices, a storefront band with signage, graffiti at street level, and pale water-tower silhouettes behind the rooflines. Streetlamps with **stepped concentric halo rings** instead of glow (PDF 024).
- **Manhattan at dusk/night:** the look this skill's example paints. Skyscraper canyons in worm's-eye with verticals converging above the frame, glass towers as vertical mullion stripes (steel blue #8a9ab0 / #5a6a80, PDF 206), masonry with punched window grids and water towers, billboards with painted graphics.
- **Mielgo rooftops (PDF 144–147):** Chinatown and Lower Manhattan roofs, water towers, verdigris domes, fire escapes, AC units, antennae; hazy pale green-grey sky (#c6cdbd); maroon brick foreground (#7a3448), ochre walls (#bbb070), slate roofs (#3a4470); stacked rectangles with irregular window grids; misregistered little rectangles and lone lime/magenta pixels as print noise; stepped pixel-staircase silhouettes. Believable signage in clean sans-serif ("Hotel THE CHEAP", "212 841…").
- **Distant skylines are flat hazy slabs** with 1–2 px window lights, no outlines, fading toward a warm haze (mauve-pink #c1a9a0 on a hazy day; PDF 050, 068). Far towers one value step darker than the sky (PDF 095).
- **Snowy night (graveyard / chase, PDF 192–201):** sky and towers #14162d → #2a2a60 with grids of small pale-blue windows; ground snow lit warm pink-magenta #975680 → #c080a0 by streetlamps, shadows #29233e; black tombstones with white snow-cap strokes; snow as white or cyan-blue (#8ad0e8) dots in 3 sizes.
- **Chase at night (Peter Chan, PDF 200):** faceted low-poly flat shading, no outlines. Sky violet #6d67af, street blue #556091, magenta buildings #c02070, burnt orange #c07040, pink billboards #f0a0d0, mint signs #a0e0c0, taxi yellow #e8c23a. Radial white streak-wedges from the vanishing point for speed; wet streets reflecting vertical light streaks; low camera.

## Brooklyn: the Morales apartment (Peter Chan, PDF 040–043)

- "Warm, raw, and lived-in"; both parents' heritage in the décor (Thompson, PDF 042).
- Sunlit wall #e8e0a8, saturated chair yellow #f5d400, bottle-green cushion #1f3a2a, honey floor #b8862a, **violet floor shadow #5a3c5a**, accent blue wall #2c4a7a.
- Surfaces cut into flat facets even when curved; some edges break into stepped rectangles; a regular halftone grid over big planes, *lighter* dots on saturated yellow. Sun shafts are translucent pale parallelograms. No black outlines, a few dark scratches.

## Aaron's apartment (Yuhki Demers, PDF 052–053)

- A refuge full of forbidden cool (throwing knives, katanas, gadgets, "an absurdly loud sound system").
- Night: blue-black #121117, slate #373b49, moonlit blue #5f6d99, oxblood chesterfield #6a1a2a, lava-lamp orange #f06020, neon panther in purple/cyan. **A fine square dot grid over shadowed brick.** Interiors mostly black, the story told by 1–3 hard parallelogram pools of window light.

## Brooklyn Visions Academy (PDF 064–069)

- The inverse of home: "clean, perfect geometric plans and shapes… very cold and sterile" (Chan). An old Brooklyn building modernised by an LA firm: "literally a bridge between two worlds" (Thompson, PDF 067).
- New = white #eae8ec, acid lime #c0d820, purple #8a5ac8, giant numerals, symmetrical one-point compositions. Old wing = orange brick #c0582a, mustard #d89a30, wood. Uniform line-up shows many body types in one regimented uniform.

## Subway: elevated station and tunnels (PDF 058–063)

- Elevated station (Neil Ross): fluorescent white #f8f8e8, steel teal-grey #5a7070, **safety yellow #e8c820** on edges, deep shadow #100d13. **Comb-edged tone transitions** (rows of short ticks instead of a gradient); streaky dry-brush stair treads; low camera, one-point perspective.
- Prowler chase keys (Zac Retz): black + indigo + violet + magenta + white flashlight; bokeh as hard flat white discs; binocular-mask POV frames in monochrome dot matrix.
- Graffiti (Patrick O'Keefe, PDF 059): lime tile #a8b870, hot-pink cloud masses #e8408a, yellow script #f0e030 outlined violet #4a3aa8 with drips, orange triangles, halftone patches that shrink to their edges, and the hero as a hollow silhouette with a dripping white outline.

## The underground depot and the Collider (PDF 082–091)

- Alice down the rabbit hole (PDF 082). Design the set as a field of cover objects so a small character can hide while a giant fights (Thompson).
- Depot (Zac Retz): navy #0c1626, teal-blue #3a6080, acid yellow-green #b8c840 railings, one pink-red spot for Spider-Man; a light-on-dark halftone grid over the whole image; containers stencilled "FISK".
- **Collider (O'Keefe):** modelled on the Large Hadron Collider, then made "more fantastical"; "a kaleidoscopic array of colors—with the bright, saturated blues, yellows, and reds reflecting off of the mirror-like gold and the chrome" (Thompson, PDF 088). Ultramarine #2030a0, violet #4030a0, gold wiring #d8a830, orange stairs #d07a20, yellow cable trays #e8d020, magenta cables #b030a0. Chrome in hard bands (white → lavender #d3d1f2 → navy #2a314e); large lighter dots in the darks; two screens half a cell apart as misregistered plates; characters reflected on the metal as multiverse copies.

## Alchemax / Doc Ock's lab (PDF 104–113)

- "A modern façade of altruism, while its true intentions are deeply sinister" (O'Keefe, PDF 104). Cold, sanitized, utilitarian, experimental, believable: "most of the early prototypes aren't actually that slick" (Thompson, PDF 114). A "subtle hall of mirrors".
- **Green tint = evil** (PDF 107): sage #9aa878, chartreuse lights #c8e83a; white/grey reflective surfaces; **flat white octagon ring lights** as outline strokes. Night exterior: slate #2e343e, fog #8a949a → #b8c0bc, flat chartreuse window rectangles #d8d040, stacked fog layers, square particles.
- Server room: near-black indigo racks with LED bars as short horizontal strokes; floor light lavender #8a80d0.

## The Hudson Valley forest (O'Keefe, PDF 116–119)

- The organic way to get Spider-Man out of the city. **Trees tripled in size but kept graphic; limbs disconnected; foliage floating**, after Watterson's *Calvin and Hobbes* (PDF 118).
- Worm's-eye: cream sun #fbe6a8, leaves #f0b040 / #e07830 / #c83a28, maroon trunks #5a1c1c as long tapered quads with a lighter side stripe; **haze goes toward cream, not blue**; characters are small dark silhouettes with a lavender rim.
- Mielgo's night forest road (PDF 104–105): purple asphalt #4a3a55, double yellow #d0a840, fir silhouettes in depth layers from pale haze to near-black, tall pale light shafts, vertical scanline texture in the masses.

## Peter Parker's hideout (Demers, O'Keefe, Rinard; PDF 126–129, 142)

- "What would a Spider-Man hideout look like if Peter had realized his full potential?" Branded swag everywhere, but a life dedicated to the city (Demers, PDF 127).
- Black void #050308 crossed by thin grey-white web strands as architecture; glossy red floor #c0204a → #e03060 with a black spider emblem and mirror reflections; floating translucent blue holo panels #5080e0; chamfered red platforms; hard red/blue split lighting (#e02030 key, #2a3a90 fill). The suit vault: ~14 lit glass cases in a curve.
- Props tell the story: Daily Bugle headlines, a key to the city, mugshots with height charts, a crayon thank-you card, an evidence board with red string.

## Aunt May's house and Queens (PDF 154–171)

- "As warm and inviting as the character herself… it smells like homemade chocolate-chip cookies with a hint of mothballs!" (Demers, PDF 154). Crucifix, wedding photos, folded flag, CRT TV, box fan, floral wallpaper.
- Loose black keylines offset 2–8 px from the forms they describe; halftone dot grids as wood shading; stepped pixel-block window light.
- Queens street (Peter Chan): pastel detached houses (lime #cfd070, grey-blue #9aacb0, mustard #c8a840, pink #e0c2b6); foliage as hundreds of small square dabs; power lines as thin black curves across the top; the Manhattan skyline behind in a coarse halftone that fades into the sky.
- **Shop fronts (O'Keefe, PDF 170):** orthographic elevations, flat light. "Nothing is really parallel or truly lines up. Some buildings have poor foundations and sink a little to one side" (PDF 168). Mismatched fonts, missing letters ("SICK SUP LIES"), AC units, chain-link roof fences. An in-joke shop: "JUSTIN K. THOMPSON P.O.S."

## Kingpin's world (Mullins, O'Keefe; PDF 174–181)

- A gilded baroque palace: cream/gold #fcfaee / #c8a040, marble, colonnade, checker floor, a suprematist art collection. Kingpin is the darkest, simplest shape in the busiest frame.
- **Columns shaded by a vertical strip of dot grid along the terminator plus one row of dark dots on the terminator line** (PDF 180). Ceiling lights and floor pools as three stepped concentric flat ellipses. Gloss on gold as crisp flat pale shapes.

## Funeral and press conference (Ruppel, Hong; PDF 094–095)

- Snowy funeral: purple sky #675469, glass towers with horizontal lavender/white window dashes, a gothic church tower uplit yellow-green #c8d060, black calligraphic tree silhouettes, snow as scattered white dots.
- Press conference at dusk: pink-mauve sky #c47c89, apricot lit facades #d79c88, purple shadow facades #473667, crowd as dark silhouettes with faint rims, **camera flashes as 4-point star sparkles** with cyan and pink spikes.
