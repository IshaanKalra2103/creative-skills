# Characters: design rules per character

Proportions, silhouettes, palettes (hex sampled from the book's scans, ±10%) and the render rules for each. **Every character keeps its own universe's rules, even in the same frame** (Lord, PDF 130). Keep a style flag per character and never unify them. Full notes and credits are in `book/`.

The standard design pipeline: 2D design (usually Shiyoon Kim) → 3D design (Omar Smith, Terry Park, Vaughan Ling) → paint-over (Wendell Dalit, Yashar Kassai, Robh Ruppel, Justin K. Thompson). The paint-over is where the look is decided.

## Miles Morales (our hero)

- **Origin:** created in 2011 by Brian Michael Bendis and Sara Pichelli for *Ultimate Spider-Man* (PDF 012); reportedly inspired by Barack Obama and Donald Glover (PDF 034). Lord and Miller only signed on if the film "didn't center on Peter Parker" (PDF 013). "Anybody can be under that mask" (Arad, PDF 012).
- **Who:** 13, half-Black, half-Puerto Rican, a smart, good-hearted kid with two present, loving parents, reluctant to follow Peter Parker (PDF 037, 203). "Peter was a boy genius, while Miles is a kid that wants to be a kid" (Beveridge, PDF 037). "Someone whose nose is pressed against the window" (Pascal, PDF 143).
- **Proportions, "baby deer":** "skinny legs with wobbly knees and… these big hands and feet" (Thompson, PDF 037). Build him about **6–6.5 heads**; limb width ≈ 0.35 × head width; hands ≈ 0.9 × head height; feet ≈ 1.1 × head height; knees slightly knocked (PDF 038). In later poses Iglesias draws him lankier, up to ~8.5 heads with very long legs (PDF 204).
- **Suit (final):** black with red web lines, a red sprayed spider on the chest, white lenses with thick black borders (red rim in some keys). Red high-top sneakers (red/white/black). Early sketches put red on shoulders, hands and soles too (Iglesias, PDF 038).
- **Lighting on the suit (PDF 112, O'Keefe):** rim light is a **hard magenta band #c040b0**, about 1–1.5% of figure width, with no falloff. The shadow side of the black suit carries a **dark plum dot grid (#3a0a2a on #10050a)**. Red parts are shaded with **parallel red hatching** along the form, like engraving.
- **Lenses are the expression.** Their shape changes with emotion; big and cartoony in the store-bought suit (PDF 193, 196).
- **Face (unmasked, PDF 073 Dalit):** lit #dda850, mid #7a3a22, shadow #4a2418; eye whites tinted by the environment (lavender #b0a8e0); thick tapered black brows; hatch strokes on the forehead; crosshatch under an eye for worry.
- **Early suits say "not ready yet":** Mielgo's home-made red/ultramarine jumpsuit with a white zip, purple cape and ochre knee pads (PDF 034); Shiyoon Kim's baggy store-bought costume with the mask pushed up like a beanie (PDF 005). The Mielgo cover (PDF 001): suit near-black violet #16131c, a **spray-stippled red shoulder** (#7f1d11 → #941b13), web lines barely lighter than the suit, lens pale grey-lavender with a thin orange-red rim.
- **Face and hair in Mielgo's concepts:** skin painted lilac-violet or plum, not brown (#69557e, #391c3c), with a warm magenta key and a cool violet-blue fill meeting at hard edges (PDF 008, 012, 035); features as tapered black strokes that don't close; **hair as scribbled spiral loops** with hatched faded sides; big ears.
- **Civilian:** hoodie over the suit, cargo shorts, high-tops, striped tube socks, headphones (PDF 036, 008). Mielgo's colourway: chartreuse jacket #b8c030, coral-red hoodie #e85040 (PDF 055).
- **Animation:** on twos while he's unsure; on ones by the Leap of Faith (video 16:30). Sticky powers are staged with camera inversion and crack ticks at the toes (PDF 070–072). Venom Strike triggers "every time he's nervous" (PDF 182).
- **Web:** a thin, wobbly, hand-drawn white line that loops into spiral lassos around the wrist or ankle, not a tube (endpapers PDF 206–209). Hand pose: the "thwip" (middle fingers folded).

## Peter B. Parker (the older Spider-Man)

- **Who:** thirty-something, jaded, out of shape, divorced, dreaming of Costa Rica; Mr. Miyagi to Miles' Karate Kid (PDF 098). Voiced by Jake Johnson.
- **Body tells history:** heroic musculature plus a beer belly; nose pushed to the side; swollen eyes; asymmetric ears **built into the model** (Kim PDF 098; Persichetti PDF 199). Stubble as short dark dashes over a blue-grey jaw shadow (#aca3a1).
- **Face paint (Ruppel, PDF 101):** a long nose as one big flat lit plane (#e0a070) against a cool shadow side; brown-olive hair (#8a7040) with halftone in its shadow masses. Bruises: flat purple black eye (#6a4a6a) with hatching; pink/red dot-screen patches.
- **Sketches:** red-pencil construction plus flat red #e03a3e and blue #2a4ab8, with white paper slivers left along lit edges (PDF 099).
- **Animation:** on ones in the forest, smooth and in control (video 16:30).

## Classic Peter Parker ("RIPeter", our universe's Spider-Man)

- Nicknamed "RIPeter" by the artists because he dies in act one (PDF 028). "The idealized version of the super hero": perfect blond hair modelled after Brad Pitt in *A River Runs Through It*, a long straight wedge nose, square jaw, big pale-blue eyes (#7080c0). Mid-twenties, Spider-Man for about ten years, married to MJ, a workshop under Aunt May's house; voiced (uncredited) by Chris Pine. "We deliver the perfect mentor to Miles only to take him away. His role is to make the next Peter that arrives feel very inadequate!" (Persichetti, PDF 028). Bendis: if Peter dies heroically enough, he becomes Miles' "Uncle Ben" (PDF 010).
- Mielgo's suit (PDF 029): red #bf506f with hard pink-white specular shapes, flat maroon #792731 and navy #121341 shadows, 3–4 value bands; **web drawn as rows of scallops** hanging between vertical lines.
- About 8 heads, athletic. Suit red #d73e44, blue #2a3aa8 (shadow #1a2078). **Web lines black in shadow, gold #f0b030 where lit**, like raised piping. Magenta rim #c050a0 on the shadow side. One lens with a gold streak, one lavender, both reflecting the environment (PDF 098).

## Spider-Gwen (Gwen Stacy)

- "Martial artist meets ballet dancer"; pointe shoes carry the idea (Belson, PDF 120). "Not just a pretty blonde girl with your typical thin princess body… tall, muscular, and powerful" (Kim, PDF 122).
- **About 8.5 heads**, tiny head, legs ≈ 55% of height; stands en pointe (PDF 120).
- **Palette (PDF 121):** suit black #0c0a10; whites #f6f4f8 with lavender shadow #d8c8e8; hood lining and underarm web panels magenta #a8286a with white web lines; pointe shoes teal #50b8c8 → #3a8ab0; blonde undercut bob.
- **Render:** black suit with a hex-mesh dot texture (#2a2830); **rim = hard lavender band #b890e0 filled with white dots, plus a thin violet line #7a4ad0 just outside it**; mask eyes are white almonds with thick magenta rims, fat at the outer top.
- Joey Chou version: a flat white shape with one hot-pink contour (#e0307a) over pink dry-brush speed streaks (PDF 122).
- Civilian: charcoal sweater with "BVA" crest, navy plaid skirt, teal ballet flats (PDF 124).

## Spider-Man Noir

- A 1930s gumshoe. "We depict him in gritty black-and-white… There's a lot of cross-hatching" (Thompson, PDF 140). "Textures that feel like an old comic book or newsprint ink" (Dalit, PDF 140).
- **Render: black silhouette + thin warm-grey slivers (#c8c4b8) on key-facing edges + hatch shadows. No colour.** Ground shadow is horizontal parallel hatching in an ellipse (PDF 138). Action frames use three values only: black, one grey marker #9a9898 laid in broad strokes, white (PDF 139).
- Fedora, trench coat flaring like a wing, goggles as two plain circles; taller than Miles (~1.3×). Voice like old-time radio; talks in period quotes (PDF 141).

## Spider-Ham (Peter Porker)

- A Looney Tunes homage; rubber-hose, "loose and graphic"; follows his own cartoon physics (portable-hole gag) (PDF 130).
- **About 2 heads tall**: huge round head, floppy triangle ears, pear body, tiny hands and feet. The snout is a second, smaller Spidey face (PDF 130).
- Red #e8404e (shade #b82838, deep #7a1a2a), blue-violet #6060c8. **Thick tapering black web lines.** Silhouette dissolves into a halftone fringe; faint twill hatch over red; halftone contact-shadow ellipse.
- Cartoon FX: circling stars, radial impact ticks, pancake squash, zig-zag shake marks (PDF 131–132).

## Peni Parker and SP//dr

- Peni: "anime through and through", about 5 heads, big brown eyes (#6a3a1a) with a big highlight, near-zero shading; black hair as a single spiky flat shape; anime symbols (anger vein, sweat drop, star eyes) (PDF 134–137).
- Uniform: white shirt, navy vest #1e2440 with a halftone dot texture (#4a5a90), pink trim #e8305a, plaid skirt, striped knee socks.
- **SP//dr:** egg/rounded-triangle head-body in red #b01e2e and blue #2e3aa8 panels; **limbs float as separate segments (magnetic levitation)** with faint violet glow in the gaps; a black visor showing emotion as red display eyes ("an infinite number of faces", Kassai PDF 134); orange halo on top; stickers and scratches.

## Kingpin (Wilson Fisk)

- **At least 8 ft tall and 7 ft wide** (Thompson, PDF 172). "Like this giant black hole that moves through space and time and swallows up everything in the room" (PDF 176). "The most abstracted CG character I've ever seen" (Lord, PDF 176).
- **Render: a pure black mass (#010104).** Only the head and hands are lit; they "float on this void". The head (≈1/9–1/10 of the height) sits below the shoulder line. White shirt V, black tie. **Rim = navy halftone band (#203257) on a 45° grid with a hair-thin pale-blue edge (#9fb5d6)** on the key side only (PDF 173).
- A special rig posed the silhouette independently of anatomy (PDF 176). Frame him so the black eclipses half the shot and bleeds off the edges.
- Face can be lit with a cool gradient (lavender #abacb4 → violet, or teal) instead of skin tone (PDF 177).

## The Prowler (Aaron Davis)

- "The low-fi Iron Man": steel claw gloves with power punches, pneumatic boots, a "giant towel-like glider cape" (Persichetti, PDF 150). Built by removing CG detail and rendering it flat, "much more graphic".
- **About 9–10 heads** with a tiny hooded head, three collar spikes, sawtooth cape hem (5–7 teeth), chunky cylinder gauntlets and boots (PDF 148).
- Navy #141336, purple #4a2e69 → #5a3a9a, black, one magenta accent (#c040a0 vents); cape in two flat values (indigo #100b29, violet #4a2a8a); hairline magenta rim #d040a0.
- **Boots leave a thin glowing magenta trail** (#ff6f9f, ~60%) along the leap arc (Mielgo, PDF 145).
- Unmasked Aaron: lean basketball-player shape, long narrow head (h:w ≈ 1.6), ~7.8 heads; black leather bomber, mustard hoodie #c8902a, olive camo cargo, wheat boots (PDF 054). He resembles his actor, Mahershala Ali (PDF 057). Café-racer bike in violet, lime and magenta with a halftone cast shadow (PDF 153).

## Doctor Octopus (Olivia Octavius)

- A real scientist "blinded by the possibilities of science"; modelled on physicists in *Particle Fever* (Kim, PDF 114). Costume "feels like something that hasn't been finished"; wild hair with purple highlights; tentacles of "synthetic semi-transparent silicon material" meant to disgust (Thompson, PDF 115).
- Final: chartreuse-lensed glasses #c8e83a, black bodysuit #1a1e18 with olive panels #5a6a30; lit green from below. **Belt-light glow drawn as a dot screen shrinking away from the source.** Translucent pale tentacles (#c8ccc0) with red and green wires inside.

## Green Goblin

- About **22 ft tall**, a monster, introduced by silhouette first like the *Jurassic Park* T-Rex (Thompson, PDF 092).
- Gorilla crouch, tiny head (≈1/6 of shoulder width), wings ≈ 2× body width. Green scales #70761f / #9ab040, purple hood #4a3a6a with a long tail, maroon wings #8a2a28, blue-violet forked tongue. **Terminator as a band of halftone dots; rim light as a row of lighter dots** (PDF 093).

## Scorpion and Tombstone

- Scorpion: "meched" — tail, mechanical arms, legs switching biped to quadruped (Persichetti, PDF 164). Rust plate armour #7a3a26, lime glowing joints #9cd23a with **halftone falloff rings instead of bloom**; the neck is wider than the head (PDF 165).
- Tombstone: Harlem-born hitman, Noir's 1930s underworld counterpart (Thompson, PDF 166). ~8.5 heads, lavender-grey skin #96949f, platinum flat-top, charcoal suit; desaturated almost to Noir's black and white.

## Jefferson Davis (Miles' father)

- Tough veteran cop with great warmth; "a wide offensive lineman football player body" (Thompson, PDF 050, 057). Looks a lot like director Peter Ramsey's father.
- ~9 heads, small head on a massive rectangular coat; shoulders ≈ 3.5 head-widths. Navy-black coat #18141f / #443641 with **halftone only in the terminator band** (PDF 051).

## Rio Morales (Miles' mother)

- A hard-working Puerto Rican nurse; an "authentic everywoman" who clearly resembles her son; "observed" from real New Yorkers (Thompson, PDF 046–047).
- ~7.5 heads; teal scrubs #6e9596; curly dark hair with a braid. Expression sheet: hair as a solid flat black mass with curl cut-outs.

## Aunt May

- "Not your father's Aunt May": feisty, bad-ass, after Lily Tomlin in *Grace and Frankie*; "She can't be too tough without showing her caring side" (Kim, PDF 158).
- ~7 heads, long neck, long legs in slim dark jeans, chin-length grey hair #9a8d82, long grey cardigan, gold necklace.

## Mary Jane Watson

- "A classic, iconic look that would be instantly recognizable": striking red hair and a jagged cut on her bangs (Thompson, PDF 096). ~8 heads; a near-black coat so the silhouette leads the eye to the hair. Freckles as orange-brown dots; beret texture as engraving-style parallel contour lines (PDF 097).

## Ganke Lee

- Miles' overachieving, oblivious roommate; role reduced (and saved for sequels) (PDF 076). ~6.8 heads, round wide-cheeked egg face, glasses; halftone dot grid only in trouser shadows. (The making-of video says it was cut back so he wouldn't read as a copy of *Homecoming*'s Ned Leeds, 13:55.)

## Vanessa Fisk

- Kingpin's late wife, his motive for the portal. Slender, ~8 heads, dark wavy bob with a white forelock streak, square-neck pencil dress (PDF 178).

## Line-up rule of thumb

| Character | Heads tall | Primitive | Render rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Miles | 6–6.5 (up to 8.5 in lanky poses) | thin vertical, big hands and feet | black suit, magenta dot rim, red hatching on red |
| Peter B. | ~8, with belly | pear on an athlete | flat planes, wear built in |
| Gwen | 8.5 | long vertical, en pointe | lavender dot rim with offset violet line |
| Noir | taller than Miles | tall coat, wing-like hem | black + grey slivers + hatching, no colour |
| Ham | 2 | circle on a pear | rubber-hose, thick tapered web lines |
| Peni | 5 | small; SP//dr is an egg | anime flat cel |
| Kingpin | ~5 | square black mass | void + navy halftone rim |
| Prowler | 9–10 | tall spike | flat indigo/violet planes, magenta accents |
| Jefferson | 9 | wide rectangle | halftone only in terminator |
| Aaron | 7.8 | tall thin rectangle | lean, long head |
