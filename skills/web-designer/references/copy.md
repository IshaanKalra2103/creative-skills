# Copy

Words are design material. Write them first (SKILL.md Step 3); layout
follows content, never the reverse.

## Headlines

- **Say the specific thing.** What a happy customer would say to a friend,
  not what a marketer would put on a billboard. "Invoices that send their own
  reminders" beats "Get paid. Forget the chasing."
- **Avoid the two-beat aphorism** ("Send the invoice. Get paid.", "Be in the
  meeting. Not in your notes."). Every generated hero is now one.
- **No italic accent word**, no gradient word (slop.md).
- Sometimes the best headline is the name and what it is: "Okra. Nigerian
  home cooking, Tuesday to Saturday, Peckham." (An example of the register;
  write your own.)
- Under 10 words on the first screen; the sub-line adds a fact, not a second
  slogan.

## Body

- Short sentences. Concrete nouns. Numbers with units. The customer's words.
- Cut every sentence that would be true of any competitor.
- One idea per paragraph; three sentences per paragraph at most on a
  marketing page.

## Buttons and links

- The button says what happens: "Send your first invoice", "See the menu",
  "Book a table", not "Get started", "Learn more", "Submit".
- One primary action per screen. A second action is a text link, not a
  ghost pill.
- Links describe their destination; never "click here".

## Data and proof

- **Specific, uneven, believable.** €1,284.37, a 23-day streak, 11 days
  sooner, 3,104 reviews. Round numbers (10,000+, 99.9%, 4.9/5) read as fake
  **[scan]**.
- **Real names from the user's world**, not Acme, Northwind, John Doe,
  Sarah Johnson **[scan]**. If you invent, invent like a novelist: Holbeck
  Bike Works, Calder Valley Print Co.
- **Testimonials only if real.** Otherwise leave them out; never fabricate
  quotes, logos or ratings for a real business.

## Microcopy

- Empty states: what will be here, and the action that fills it.
- Errors: what happened, what to do, no blame, no "Oops!".
- Confirmations: the object's name ("Delete 'checkout.new-payment-sheet'?").
- Sentence case everywhere. Full stops, not exclamation marks **[scan]**.
- Em dashes are the punctuation of generated prose **[scan]**: a full stop,
  a colon or a comma usually reads cleaner.
- No emoji in interface copy **[scan]**. No ✨ next to AI features: name
  what it does ("Summary", "Suggested reply").

## Voice

Write a voice line in DIRECTION.md ("Talks like a good shopkeeper: brief,
warm, exact about the beans"). Every string passes through it. Read the page
aloud; anything you would not say to a person, rewrite.
