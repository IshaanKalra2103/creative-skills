# Copy and content

Half of what reads as "AI design" is AI writing. Generic words and perfect
fake data make a beautiful layout look generated.

## 1. Write the content before the layout

Before drawing a screen, write out exactly what is on it: every label, every
row, every number, every name. Real content decides the layout (how long are
the titles? how many items? what is the biggest number?). Layout first means
content gets bent to fit placeholder shapes, and the result looks like a
template.

## 2. The voice

- **Labels say what things are.** "Add habit", "Transfer", "Save recipe".
  Verbs on buttons, nouns on screens.
- **Sentence case.** Everywhere except proper nouns.
- **Short.** A button is one to three words. An empty state is one sentence
  and one action.
- **Specific over clever.** "No spending since Tuesday" beats "You're crushing
  it!".
- **Calm.** No exclamation marks in system copy. No emoji. No "Oops!".
- **One personality, if any,** and it lives in a few chosen places (an
  empty state, a milestone), not every label.

**Banned phrases** (the scan catches most): effortless, seamless, unlock,
elevate, supercharge, empower, journey, level up, all in one place, at your
fingertips, take control, stay on track, welcome back, let's get started,
like never before, transform your, AI-powered.

## 3. Believable data

Specific data is the cheapest premium signal available.

| Slop | Believable |
|---|---|
| $1,000.00 | $1,284.37 |
| 1,234 steps | 8,612 steps |
| 12-day streak | 23 days, best was 41 |
| 75% complete | 5 of 7 |
| John Doe | Mara Okafor |
| Coffee Shop, $4.50 | Bluestone Lane, $6.25 |
| Lorem ipsum | the real sentence |

- Uneven numbers, real-sounding names from the user's region, real places,
  real brand-shaped merchant names (invented, not trademarks).
- Show a realistic **state**: some things done, one overdue, one new. Not
  everything perfect, not everything empty.
- Dates and times relative where people think relatively ("Yesterday",
  "In 2 hours"), absolute where they plan.

## 4. Empty, error, loading

These states are where crafted apps show care.

- **Empty:** what will be here, and the one action that fills it. No
  illustration of a sad cloud.
- **Error:** what happened, in plain words, and what to do. Never "Something
  went wrong".
- **Loading:** skeletons that match the real layout, or nothing for anything
  under 300ms.
- **Permissions:** ask in context, at the moment the feature needs it, with
  a sentence about why.
