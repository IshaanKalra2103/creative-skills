# The Lantern Ghost (灯の子)

Premise: a small ghost rises out of the shadow under an old tree and brings the morning's colour to the world with it.

- The change: a grey, colourless hour before dawn, to a valley full of colour and light.
- The signature moment: the ghost comes apart into a gust of petals and light that pours across the valley as the sun crests.
- The witness: a stone lantern at the foot of the tree (unlit, then lit) and a wilted bellflower beside it.
