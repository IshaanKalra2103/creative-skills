# Trailer brief

Make a 60–75 second action-game trailer feel deliberately directed rather than captured from raw gameplay. `board.json` is this brief as a 12-shot plan; `assets/reference_storyboard.png` is what it looks like drawn.

## Camera notes
- keep the horizon stable during traversal;
- lead the hero into negative space;
- cut instead of making huge corrective camera orbits;
- reserve the strongest low angle for the villain/boss;
- keep the final two-shot clean and continuity-safe.
